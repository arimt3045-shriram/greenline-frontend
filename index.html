    Eligibility, Lateral Entry, Anti-Ragging & Helpline

    Admission Norms

    Eligibility & Lateral Entry Rules

    • 3-Year Diploma (Regular): Passed Class X (Matriculation) with at least 35% aggregate; Mathematics and Science compulsory. Minimum age 15 years (no upper age cap for general autonomous seats).
    • 2-Year Lateral Entry (2nd Year): Passed Class XII / Intermediate Science or Vocational (Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics) OR 2-Year ITI after Class X (in current year or preceding 2 years).
    • Residential / Jharkhand Lateral Entry Age Cap: As of 30 June of admission year: ≤ 20 years (General & OBC) and ≤ 25 years (ST, SC & PT). Candidate must hold Jharkhand domicile for state-venture residential seats.
    • Merit Weightage (Lateral Entry): 50% Written Test & Personal Interview + 20% Class X Board Marks + 30% Class XII / ITI Marks.
    ITI Trade Background Permitted Diploma Branches
    Circuital (Computer / Electronics / Electrical) Computer Science, ECE, Electrical & Electronics
    Mechanical or Equivalent Trade Mechanical, Automobile, Civil Engineering
    Class XII Science / Vocational (PCM) All 6 Engineering Branches Permitted
    Physical Verification

    Mandatory Documents & Anti-Ragging Procedure

    • Original & attested photocopies of Class X Marksheet, Class XII / ITI Marksheet (Lateral Entry), Residential/Domicile Certificate, and Caste Certificate (OBC/SC/ST/PT).
    • Transfer Certificate, Character Certificate, Medical Fitness Certificate (examined by Institute Medical Officer), Aadhaar ID, Passport Photos, and printed Application Form.
    • Mandatory 75% Attendance & Conduct: 75% minimum attendance in lectures, tutorials, and practicals per subject is compulsory. Zero tolerance for tobacco, alcohol, or ragging.
    3-Step UGC Anti-Ragging Affidavit Procedure

    Step 1: Visit www.ANTIRAGGING.in or www.AMANMOVEMENT.org and select Online Affidavits.
    Step 2: Complete the electronic form with student and parent/guardian contact details.
    Step 3: Print both emailed PDFs (Student Affidavit & Parent Affidavit), sign both, and submit at admission.
